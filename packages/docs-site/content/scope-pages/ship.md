# Ship

A ship is an individual spaceborne unit or a carrier represented through ship script. Ship
operations cover damage and repair, movement, design and growth state, flags, and, for carriers,
related colony or planet operations. It is the unit-level context within a fleet, with the generated
table defining which operations apply to this broad game scope.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `ship_event` bodies and links from planets, starbases, colonies, or
other ships.
