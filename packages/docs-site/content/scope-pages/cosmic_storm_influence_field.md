# Cosmic storm influence field

A cosmic storm influence field is an area centered on a galactic object and covering affected
systems. Scripts can iterate the systems in that field, apply modifiers, and recalculate its
influence. It describes the storm's area of effect and remains distinct from the moving storm
object itself.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `cosmic_storm_influence_field_event` bodies, field-creation callbacks,
and the `lastCreatedCosmicStormInfluenceField` link.
