A **bombardment stance** is a fleet's policy for orbital bombardment. It
controls which fleets can select the stance, when bombardment stops, damage to
the planet and armies, population loss or abduction, surrender, and AI choice.
The SDK writes stances under `common/bombardment_stances/`.

## Gate the stance from fleet scope

`trigger` is required and runs on the fleet. The example follows vanilla's
selective stance by checking a policy flag on the fleet owner:

<PairedExample name="bombardment-stances" />

`default` is also required. Set it to true only for the one stance new fleets
should start with in the complete set available to your mod. The SDK does not
check that the loaded definitions have exactly one default.

## Damage and population fields use different scales

`planetDamage` and `armyDamage` are general damage multipliers. `planetDamage`
can also be a weight block evaluated from the fleet with the bombarded planet
as `FROM`. `killPopChance` is the chance applied when planetary damage reaches
the game's threshold, and `killPopAmount` gives the minimum and maximum amount
killed or abducted in one occurrence.

`minPopsToKillPop` stops population loss or abduction at or below the supplied
amount. The rules do not define that amount's unit. Current vanilla uses `2100`
for selective bombardment and `1100` for indiscriminate bombardment; start from
a current comparable stance and verify the threshold in game.

`stopWhenArmiesDead`, `stopWhenGroundCombat`, and `acceptSurrender` are
independent. A stance can stop when defenses fall, stop while ground combat is
happening, and accept surrender, or choose a different combination.
`abductPops` changes the population outcome instead of changing the damage
multipliers.

## AI weight can inspect the target planet

`aiWeight` is required. It runs from fleet scope with the bombarded planet as
`FROM`, so modifiers can consider the target as well as the fleet and its
owner. Vanilla lowers the weight sharply when a target should not be treated
as a hostile defended planet.

<Callout type="warn" title="The stance id also selects UI art">
  Stellaris derives bombardment buttons from the stance id, including
  `GFX_fleet_order_button_ground_support_<id>` and its `_selected` variant. The registry has no
  graphics field, and the SDK does not check that those sprites exist.
</Callout>

Test fleet eligibility, the default on new fleets, button art, stance changes,
all stop and surrender paths, planet and army damage, population thresholds,
abduction, AI selection, ownership changes, and save/load in Stellaris. See
[Testing your mod](/guides/testing-your-mod/).
