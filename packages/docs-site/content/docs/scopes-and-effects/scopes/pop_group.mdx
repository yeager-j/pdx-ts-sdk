A pop group is a grouped population context. Scripts can change its amount or species, clear its
ethics, force faction evaluation, and inspect traits associated with its species. It is the local
population unit used for demographic, employment, and faction behavior without representing a
whole species.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `pop_group_event` bodies and links from armies, planets, ships, or
countries that expose a related pop group.
