A sector is a regional context that groups an empire's space and colonies. Sector methods iterate
owned colonies, planets, systems, and pre-FTL countries within its borders, and can store sector
flags. It is the administrative view between an individual system and the country that owns the
region.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include the `sector` link, sector iterators, and typed callbacks; for leaders,
the documented sector is their current location rather than necessarily their governor assignment.
