A species is the context for a biological or constructed population identity and its
characteristics. Scripts can change its portrait and characteristics, manage rights and controls,
inspect traits, and iterate its pop groups. It is the identity-level view shared by populations
across worlds rather than one local group.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include species links from countries, ships, pop groups, leaders, and armies,
plus callbacks that expose founder, growing, assembling, or declining species.
