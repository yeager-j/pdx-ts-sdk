An agreement is the diplomatic contract between an overlord and a subject. Scripts can set its
preset and terms, adjust loyalty, and control rules for integration, diplomacy, expansion, war,
access, and sensors. It provides the mechanical frame for the permissions and obligations of a
subject relationship.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `agreement_event` bodies and typed callbacks that supply an
`AgreementScope`; other paths depend on the enclosing script contract.
