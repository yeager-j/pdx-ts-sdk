A pop faction is a political group formed by part of an empire's population. Scripts can inspect
its approval, support, type, and parameters, mark its extorted state, and store permanent or timed faction
flags. It is the faction-level view of domestic politics rather than an individual pop group or
the country as a whole.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `pop_faction_event` bodies, faction iterators, and links from a pop
group or leader to its faction.
