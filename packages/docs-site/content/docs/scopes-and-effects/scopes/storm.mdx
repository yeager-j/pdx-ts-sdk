Storm represents a cosmic storm and the systems in its influence. Scripts can iterate systems
added to, removed from, or currently within the storm, store storm flags, and lock the storm in
place. It is the moving weather-like galactic phenomenon, distinct from its separate influence-field
context.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include `cosmic_storm_event` bodies and the `lastCreatedCosmicStorm` link after
an effect creates a storm.
