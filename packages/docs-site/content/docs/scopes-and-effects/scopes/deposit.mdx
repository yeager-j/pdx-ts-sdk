A deposit is a localized resource or feature attached to a planet. Scripts can clear its blocker,
remove it, and store permanent or timed flags on it. It is the feature-level context beneath the
broader colony or planet flow that creates and manages planetary deposits.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include deposit-creation callbacks and the `lastAddedDeposit` link from
supported planetary contexts.
