A mission is an observation-post mission or contract issued by a country. Scripts can inspect its
type, store mission flags, and cancel the contract with the supported refund and message options.
It represents one assigned objective within a country's wider observation and contract activity.

## Common entry points

Common entry points include country mission iterators and typed callbacks that supply a
`MissionScope`.
